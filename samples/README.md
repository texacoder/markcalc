# Test pack for Mark Calculator

A small General Science test (20 marks) with three answer sheets of different quality, and the marks a teacher would give each one. Use it to check that the site works and to see how close its marks come to a teacher's.

| File | What it is | Used in step |
|---|---|---|
| `question-paper.pdf` | Typed question paper, 5 questions, 20 marks | 3 |
| `marking-scheme.pdf` | Typed marking scheme with the mark split | 2 |
| `answer-sheet-1-priya.jpg` | Handwritten, **good** answers | 5 |
| `answer-sheet-2-rahul.jpg` | Handwritten, **average** answers (labels missing, unbalanced equation…) | 5 |
| `answer-sheet-2-rahul-scanned.pdf` | Same sheet as a scanned PDF (tests the PDF path) | 5 |
| `answer-sheet-3-anu.jpg` | Handwritten, **weak** answers, one question not attempted | 5 |
| `answer-sheet-1-priya.txt` | Priya's answers as typed text (tests the typed path) | 5 |

The answer sheets are drawn with a handwriting font. Real handwriting is harder to read, so real results may be a little less accurate.

## What to enter on the site

1. **Exam details:** Subject `General Science` · Class / course `Class 10` · Total marks `20`
   · Syllabus (optional): `Light – refraction; Motion – speed and Newton's laws; Electricity – simple circuits; Life processes – photosynthesis`
2. **Marking scheme:** *Or upload the marking scheme* → `marking-scheme.pdf`. Its text is copied into the box; check it's there.
3. **Question paper:** *Or upload the question paper* → `question-paper.pdf`. Its text is copied into the box.
4. **How should it be checked:** tick **Step marks** and **Ignore spelling & grammar**.
5. **Answer sheet:** upload `answer-sheet-1-priya.jpg` and click **Calculate marks**. You can leave the name and roll number empty, because they're read from the sheet.
   Then click **Mark the next student** and do the same with `answer-sheet-2-rahul.jpg` (or the scanned PDF) and `answer-sheet-3-anu.jpg`.

Each marking uses only **1 page image**. The question paper and scheme are typed PDFs, so they become text.

**How to judge the result:** partial marks involve judgement, so two teachers can also differ a little.
- **Good:** each total within **±1** of the table.
- **Acceptable:** within ±2.
- **A problem:** more than ±2 off, or a clearly wrong reading of the handwriting (check the "Student's answer" column).
